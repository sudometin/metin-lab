## What they did

An intracortical BCI decoded attempted handwriting movements into text in real time, with a recurrent network and a language model on top.

## The lesson for sensory interfaces

Pick a behavior with rich, varied neural trajectories. Distinct patterns are easier to decode than similar ones. Odors with distinct glomerular maps should work the same way.
