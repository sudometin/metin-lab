## The question

How can a nose tell apart so many different molecules? Before 1991 nobody knew what the receptors even were.

## What they did

Buck and Axel looked for genes that were expressed only in the olfactory epithelium and looked like G-protein-coupled receptors. They found a large, diverse family of them, which later turned out to be the biggest gene family in the mammalian genome.

## Why I care

Every decoding model I build sits on top of this combinatorial code: many receptor types, each tuned broadly, each odor activating a pattern. The **Odor Decoder** game on the home page is a toy version of exactly that.
