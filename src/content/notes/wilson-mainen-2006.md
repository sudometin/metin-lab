## Why read it

A review that walks through the first stages of the olfactory pathway in flies and mammals and asks what computation each stage performs.

## What I took away

- Convergence onto glomeruli boosts signal-to-noise.
- Local circuits in the bulb reshape, normalize and synchronize the input before it reaches cortex.

## For students

Read this before any recent paper on olfactory coding. It gives the vocabulary.
