## The question

Is smell slow, integrating evidence over many sniffs, or fast?

## What they did

Rats performed an odor discrimination task while the researchers measured how long they sampled. Even for hard mixtures, sampling time stayed short and accuracy did not improve much with longer sampling.

## Why I care

If the animal decides within about one sniff, a decoder working on neural recordings should be able to as well. That sets the time window I use for classification.

## Open questions

- Which features of the first sniff carry most of the information: timing, rate or oscillation phase?
